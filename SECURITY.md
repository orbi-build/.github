# Security policy

Please don't report security vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Report a vulnerability

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:

https://github.com/orbi-build/orbi/security/advisories/new

This covers the Orbi engine, Orbi Cloud (orbi.build), and the other orbi-build repositories. Only the maintainers can see your report. If your finding is about one of our other public repositories, you can also open the report from that repository's Security tab.

Helpful details:

- what is affected (repository, version or commit, or the orbi.build page or API)
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- what an attacker could do with it

We'll reply within 3 business days, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you in the advisory unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

Please test only against your own accounts and repositories, and don't access other users' data.
